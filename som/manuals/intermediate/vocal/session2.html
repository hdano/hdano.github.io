For these exercises, get an instrument for tune reference, such as piano or guitar

Semitones

  1. Sing “aah” in the tune of middle C
  2. Then step up one semitone to C#
  3. Continue stepping up until higher C octave
  4. Then step down one semitone at a time until you got back to the middle C

Tones

  1. Sing “aah” in the tune of middle C
  2. Then step up one tone to D
  3. Continue stepping up in tones (E F# G# A# C) until higher C octave (Note: This is not the solfasyllable so it should sound differently)
  4. Then step down one tone at a time until you got back to the middle C

Octaves

  1. Sing “aah” in the tune of middle C
  2. Then step up one octave to higher C
  3. Optionally, try reaching the next higher C then back to the previous higher C
  4. Get back to the middle C
  5. Then step down one octave to lower C
  6. Optionally, try reaching the next lower C then back to the previous lower C

Finally, try singing these intervals continuously so you can understand their differences.

Major Scale

Do – Re – Mi – Fa – Sol – La – Ti – Do

Sing the following syllables in the tune these major scales in ascending and descending order:

  1. C major C D E F G A B C
  2. D major D E F# G A B C# D
  3. E major E F# G# A B C# Eb E
  4. F major F G A Bb C D E F
  5. G major G A B C D E F# G
  6. A major A B C# D E F# G# A
  7. B major B C# Eb E F# G# Bb B

Using the C major scale, try changing the order of the syllables:

  1. Do – Mi – Re – Fa – Mi – Sol – Fa – La – Sol – Ti – La – Do(high)
  2. Mi – Sol – Re – Do – Ti – Do(high) – La
  3. Do – Ti – Re – Do(high) – Mi – La – Sol – Fa

Minor Scale

La – Ti – Do – Re – Mi – Fa – Sol – La

Sing the following syllables in the tune these minor scales:

  1. C minor C D Eb F G A Bb C
  2. D minor D E F G A Bb C D
  3. E minor E F# G A B C D E
  4. F minor F G G# Bb C C# Eb F
  5. G minor G A Bb C D Eb F G
  6. A minor A B C D E F G A
  7. B minor B C# D E F# G A B
