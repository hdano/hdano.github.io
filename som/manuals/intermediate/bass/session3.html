All musicians, whether you are a singer, drummer, keyboardist, guitarist or bassist, need to practice with a metronome. Metronome will help you play with correct timing for each part of the song you are playing. It will also improve your inner clock that allows you to play in time even without metronome or in low volume.

Metronome devices are available in music stores or you can download Metronome apps into your phone. There are also online metronome where you can play via your device's internet browser, just search for "Online Metronome".

When playing bass exercise, set the metronome to moderate slow tempo, like 80 - 100 bpm. Then when you are confident enough, set it to faster tempo. Just make sure you are playing the exercises correctly being honest to yourself.

When playing bass for a song, use the correct tempo so you can easily get along with the whole band during rehearsal and in actual worship session.
