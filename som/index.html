About AHMUZICS

AHMUZICS is a church-schooling system under Amazing Hope Christian Church International to equip the music ministers with music knowledge that will help them be more efficient in the music ministry. This is open for members of AHCCI who has undergone the discipleship journey and has a heart to serve God through music.

AHMUZICS was designed to be held for each quarter of the year (e.i. every 3 months), but because of conflicts with other church activities such as Life Retreat, we made the schedule flexible enough to give way for them. While the schedule is still changing, we will post announcements during the services or via your network leaders, and also in this page.

AHMUSICS courses have two levels - Intermediate and Advance. Unfortunately, we do not have basic level for we expect student to have basic knowledge of instruments or singing before enrolling so they can easily comprehend the topics along with other students.

For each level, we have following classes:

  • Guitar
  • Bass Guitar
  • Keyboard
  • Drums
  • Technical Voicing

When a student passes the intermediate level, he/she will qualify to take the advanced level. After passing the advance level, students will move on to either be a member of the music team as trainee or if already a member, continue to be used in the music ministry with enhanced skills and knowledge.

Registration fee is changing from time to time, but it will cost not more than the learning you will acquire from our lessons. "Money can be stolen, but knowledge cannot be taken away from you"

For more information, please visit our AHMUZICS facebook page: facebook.com/@ahmuzics
